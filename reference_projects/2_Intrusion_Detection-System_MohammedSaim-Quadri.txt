WHAT IT DOES
- End-to-end MLOps pipeline on CICIDS2018: stratified sampling, XGBoost (CPU) and CNN+LSTM (GPU), SHAP explanations, Docker.
- Builds a stratified, memory-safe sample with create_master_dataset.py; the full data is not stored in the repo.

RESULTS REPORTED
- XGBoost: accuracy 98.43%, F1 97.96%, balanced accuracy 78.11%.
- CNN+LSTM: accuracy 96.31%, balanced accuracy 55.16% (it predicts Benign for almost everything).
- XGBoost: 0% recall on the Infilteration class, despite high accuracy.

LIVE / REAL-TIME
- No. Connecting predictions to live capture with CICFlowMeter is listed as future work.

HOW IT HANDLES THE ISSUES
- Imbalance: stratified sampling. SMOTE/GAN not used yet (future work).
- Distribution shift: not documented.
- Thresholds and normalization: not detailed.

LIMITATIONS
- Accuracy hides per-class failure: Infilteration recall is 0%.
- The CNN collapses to the majority class.

RELEVANCE TO OUR PROBLEM
- Same warning as ours: high accuracy can hide a class the model never detects. Our Infilteration result (mostly called Benign) is the same kind of failure.
- Per-class recall should be reported next to accuracy in every result.
